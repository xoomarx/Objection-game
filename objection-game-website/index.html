How to Play

The Goal. Win cases by demolishing your opponent's credibility, swaying the jury, and surviving the judge's patience.

Prep. Pick the right evidence for your case — every piece has strength and risk.

Investigate. Visit locations to find clues and reveal statement weaknesses.

Negotiate. Pressure or charm opposing counsel into a settlement, or take it to trial.

Courtroom Combat. Each witness statement has a hidden weakness — match the right evidence or the right objection.

  • Present Evidence — match the statement's weakness for a devastating hit.
  • Object — Relevance, Hearsay, or Speculation. Wrong type angers the judge.
  • Cross-Examine — drains witness confidence; low confidence reveals weaknesses.
  • Pressure — high risk, depends on Intimidation.
  • Calm Clarification — safe credibility gain.
  • Recess — limited; restores confidence and judge patience.
  • Special — your style's signature power move.
  • Closing Argument — finishing move. End the trial when ahead.

New Systems. Build Focus and Combo with smart play. Spend Focus on Consult Notes, Pin Down, Expose Contradiction, and Dramatic Reveal. When the opponent hits you hard, Adrenaline builds toward Second Chair Save.

Career Perks. Winning cases can unlock perks like Bench Whisperer, Evidence Hawk, and Jury Magnet. Reputation also gives Star Power in later trials.

Voices. Witnesses, opposing counsel, and courtroom moments can be spoken aloud. Use the Voice button in the top bar to toggle speech.

New Fun Upgrades. Use Snap Objection for a faster objection window, discover Evidence Combos, meet stronger rival lawyers, and unlock saveable career progress.

Audio. The courtroom now has layered ambience, judge voice lines, crowd reactions, gavel slams, case-closed stings, witness voices, and dramatic objection hits. All sounds are original/generated.

Difficulty. Choose Story, Associate, Partner, or Legendary from the main menu.

Keyboard: 1–9 for actions, Space to advance, Esc for menu.
